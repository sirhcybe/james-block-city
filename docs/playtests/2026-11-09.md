## What we found when we looked at the car controls
We tested this in code (`LaneDriver` with `screenDirToGrid`), holding one direction for 6 seconds,
starting on the Downtown road heading down-right on screen:

- **Holding Up:** the car does a U-turn straight away. That part is fine. Then at the next corner
  it **gets stuck flipping back and forth forever**, alternating between two directions on the
  same node.
- **Holding Left:** same thing. It U-turns, then flips back and forth forever at a corner.
- **Holding Down or Right:** it drives normally and turns at corners.
- **Holding Up-right:** it wanders all the way round the road loop.

Why it happens:
- The roads run diagonally on screen, so a single arrow key (or a thumb pushed straight up) sits
  exactly between two road directions.
- The car turns around whenever the stick is more than about 127° from where it's heading. After
  it goes round a corner, the same held key is suddenly "behind" it, so it turns round. It comes
  back to the corner, turns again, and so on.

Other things that probably add to "doesn't feel right":
- Turns are instant 90° snaps at a point. The car picture jumps between 4 facings, and there's
  no curve through the corner. The GDD says road assist "rounds corners".
- A U-turn mid-road is an instant flip on the spot.
- The car stops dead at T-junctions when no direction is held.
