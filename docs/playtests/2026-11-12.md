# Playtest 2026-11-12 — after spec 007

**Who:** Chris and James, on the build after spec 007 (finale, PWA).

## What Chris said
1. **Firefighter mission bug.** Got stuck on the firefighter mission that needs a police scooter.
   Getting the scooter did not finish the objective.
2. **Minigames.** They need more fun, progression and a moderate challenge (for example a small
   failure state in Ducks Crossing). Show the score on screen and a picture of how far along you
   are.
3. **World and finding your way.** Make the map about 50% bigger so things have more room. Show
   arrows that point at the current objective (for example the flags) when the player is stuck.
4. **Water and rescues.** Once the dock is built, let the player drive a boat, with rescue
   missions for firefighters or police.
5. **Builder.** Replace the building mechanic with a minigame where you drag materials into place
   and pick the fasteners.
6. **Home screen.** It could use an update.

Along the way: improve the art and the way things play across the board.

## What this led to
Spec `specs/008-playtest-3`.

## What we found and fixed
1. **Stuck firefighter.** Every boss used the Cop's line: "You need a police bike". The fire
   chief said it too, but a firefighter's Shop only shows the ladder, and Fire's Pro missions wait
   for the ladder, so getting a bike changed nothing. Each boss now names their own upgrade, the
   arrow points at the Shop while that is the only thing to do, and buying it says "go and see
   your chief".
2. **Minigames.** One shared top bar (score, star meter, time, hearts), levels inside a round,
   streaks, and hearts with a friendly "Oops, try again". In Traffic Helper, going green while
   someone is still crossing costs a heart.
3. **Map and arrows.** City 48 × 32 → 60 × 40 tiles, plus open sea (72 × 48 in all). Objective
   arrows after 5 s without progress; flags have light beams; a step tracker sits on the HUD.
4. **Boat.** Dock It moors a rescue boat; a lifeguard gives two firefighter and two police
   rescues.
5. **Builder.** The blaster opens Build It!: drag materials to the blueprint, pick the tool.
6. **Home screen.** Skyline, the team by their vehicles, the sea, the name in blocks.
