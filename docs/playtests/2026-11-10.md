# Playtest 2026-11-10 — driving after spec 003 part A

**Who:** Chris, on the live GitHub Pages build (spec 003 T1–T4).
**Covers:** spec 003 T5 / AC5.

## What Chris said
- "Driving is better but it's pretty hard to travel between the two city blocks."

## What we found
- **One road between the blocks.** Downtown and the Plaza were joined by a single road in the
  middle of the seam. The top and bottom ring roads stopped at grass.
- **A diagonal push could park the car for good.** On a road running 108° from the stick (for
  example Down-Right held on a road going down-left), the stick was:
  - too far off to keep driving (100°);
  - not far enough to U-turn (110°);
  - just outside the "go back from a stop" limit (70°; the way back was 72°).
  So the car stopped and never moved, while the push pointed straight at the other block.

## Fixed
- Top and bottom ring roads now cross the seam, so there are three roads between the blocks.
- Going back from a stop is allowed within 85° (still under 90°, so it can't bounce between the
  two ends of a road). ADR-004 updated.
- New unit tests: holding Right or Down-Right from every node and heading in Downtown reaches the
  Plaza, and holding Left or Up-Left from the Plaza reaches Downtown. The AC1 flip-flop sweep
  still passes.

## Result for AC5
Better, with the travel fix above. Chris chose to carry on to the rest of 003, 004 and 005 without
another stop; he'll re-check driving in the next playtest.
