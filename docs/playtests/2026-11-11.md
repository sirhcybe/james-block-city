# Playtest 2026-11-11 — polish round after spec 005

**Who:** Chris, on the build after spec 005 (Builder).

## What Chris said
1. Driving controls are still not right. It's very hard to turn corners a lot of the time.
2. After playing a minigame, sometimes the character just walked to the right with no button
   pressed.
3. There's no way to quit a minigame once it's started.
4. The police officer could play the firefighter minigames.
5. The tap targets were off in the put-out-the-flames minigame.
6. The ladder didn't go up in the raise-the-ladder minigame.

## What we found and fixed
1. **Turning.** A single arrow key could almost never turn: ties went straight on, Up while heading
   down-right spun the car round, and a turn pressed in the last tile was ignored. Now a changed
   key turns on a tie, a turn wish carries to a junction up to 3 nodes ahead, and late turns are
   taken on a tighter curve. Simulated turn success went from 0–23% to 85–100%. See ADR-008.
2. **Walking on its own.** If you press the mouse on the world and let go over a HUD button (Action
   is bottom-right), the HUD keeps the "up". The city stayed in hold-to-steer, so the player kept
   walking toward the pointer. The touch joystick could stick the same way. The city now checks
   each frame which pointers are really down. As a safety net it also lets go of every key and
   pointer when it pauses for a minigame or menu and when it resumes. We didn't catch the exact
   minigame sequence in the act. This was the one way we found to walk on its own, and it matches
   "to the right".
3. **Quitting.** Every minigame has the blue back arrow bottom-left, the same as the menus (and Esc
   on a keyboard). It goes straight back to the city with no coins and no fuss. Taps on it never
   reach the game underneath.
4. **Other jobs' games.** Pads for another job's minigames are faded and still. Standing on one
   says "That game is for another job…" once and doesn't start it.
5. **Window Splash taps.** The tap target was the flame sprite. It starts at scale 0, and its hit
   box was in unscaled texture pixels, so taps often missed. Now each whole window cell is the
   target. The kitchen flames got fixed, generous targets too, because they shrink after the
   first squirt.
6. **Ladder Rescue.** A quick tap (what a 6-year-old does) raised the ladder about 0.2 floors and
   counted as a miss. Presses during the 0.8 s reset were dropped. Now a tap starts the ladder
   rising and the next tap stops it, and holding and letting go still works. A press during the
   reset is kept. A rising ladder that reaches the top stops by itself. The ladder is drawn with
   rails and rungs, swinging up from a turntable on the truck.

## Tests
- Unit: single-key turning at every junction; input reset; dropping released pointers; ladder tap,
  queued press, and auto-stop.
- e2e (`tests/e2e/polish.spec.ts`): no walking on your own after a minigame (key held, click and
  tap closes); releasing the mouse over the Action button stops steering; the cop can't start
  Window Splash; the back arrow leaves three minigames with no coins; Esc leaves; Window Splash
  corner taps score; one tap raises the ladder.
